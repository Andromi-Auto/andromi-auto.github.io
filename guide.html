checklistRequirements

  • check_circle
    HyperOS RequiredA Xiaomi phone on HyperOS. Andromi Auto relies on HyperOS's Xiaomi Interconnectivity to appear on the car screen.
  • check_circle
    Global or EEA (EU) ROM RecommendedAndroid Auto runs on the phone, so it needs Google services and a ROM where Android Auto is supported. Global and EEA ROMs have both.
  • check_circle
    Android Auto RequiredThe Android Auto app from Google Play, kept up to date.
  • check_circle
    Unlocked bootloader and root RequiredRoot with KernelSU or KernelSU Next is needed to install the Interconnectivity module, and lets the app set up automatic start with one tap. Unlocking the bootloader erases the phone, so back up first.
  • check_circle
    Metamodule RequiredOur KernelSU modules add apps and files to the system, which KernelSU only does with a metamodule installed. We recommend NoMount. See Metamodule.
  • check_circle
    Interconnectivity module RequiredGlobal and EEA ROMs cannot connect to a Xiaomi EV on their own. This KernelSU module adds it; without it, Andromi Auto has no car screen to show on. See Interconnectivity module.
  • key
    Digital key module OptionalNot needed for Andromi Auto. See Car digital key.
verified

Tested on: Xiaomi 17 Pro Max, HyperOS EU (EEA) ROM, KernelSU Next with the NoMount metamodule.

info

Andromi Auto installs under the name com.baidu.searchbox, because the car only shows apps from Xiaomi's allowed list. Uninstall the real Baidu app first, if you have it. Why this name?

downloadInstall

  1. Download the APK

    On your phone, open the latest release and tap Andromi-Auto-release.apk, or use the Download button on the home page.

  2. Install it

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps, then tap Install.

  3. Open Andromi Auto

    The setup walks you through language, look and the permissions the app needs. Allow them all, so Android Auto can use the microphone, notifications and Bluetooth.

play_circleFirst start

  1. Tap Start Android Auto

    On the home screen, tap the big Start Android Auto button. The first time, Android Auto may ask you to accept its terms and permissions on the phone. Accept them.

  2. Check that it works on the phone

    Android Auto opens full screen inside Andromi Auto. Try Maps or music to be sure everything works before you go to the car.

  3. Go back

    The app has no back arrows. Use your phone's back gesture to leave Android Auto or a settings screen. Swipe back twice on the home screen to close the app completely.

directions_carShow it on the car

  1. Connect the phone to the car

    Connect your phone to the car with Xiaomi Interconnectivity as you normally do, so the car can show your phone's apps.

  2. Open Andromi Auto on the car screen

    Find Andromi Auto in the car's list of phone apps (it may show the Baidu name or icon on some car software) and open it.

  3. Pin it in Portrait

    On the Andromi Auto home screen, set the Screen switch to Portrait, then pin the window on the car screen where you want it.

  4. Switch to Landscape

    Once it is pinned, set the Screen switch to Landscape and start Android Auto.

info

The exact menus on the car depend on its software version. If Andromi Auto is not in the list, check that the phone is connected and, on a Global or EEA ROM, that the Interconnectivity module is installed.

boltAutomatic start

With automatic start, Android Auto starts by itself as soon as the phone connects to the car's Bluetooth, and stops again when the car disconnects. Open it from the home screen: tap the Automatic start card.

  1. Root setup

    Tap Set up with root and allow the root request in KernelSU. This re-enables the part of Android Auto 17.4+ that lets an app start it, and gives Andromi Auto the permissions it needs.

    If the button does not work, run these commands once in a root shell (also shown in the app, with a Copy button):

    pm enable --user 0 com.google.android.projection.gearhead/com.google.android.apps.auto.wireless.setup.receiver.WirelessStartupReceiver
appops set --user 0 com.baidu.searchbox SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW allow
pm grant com.baidu.searchbox android.permission.BLUETOOTH_CONNECT
pm grant com.baidu.searchbox android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
  2. Car Bluetooth

    Tap Choose device and pick your car from the paired Bluetooth devices.

  3. Open from the background

    Allowed by the root setup. Otherwise tap Allow and turn on "Display over other apps" for Andromi Auto.

  4. Turn it on

    Switch on Start Android Auto automatically. When all three items show a check mark, it is ready.

What happens in the car

  • bluetooth_connected
    The car connectsAndromi Auto opens by itself and starts Android Auto, without the start screen.
  • open_in_new
    You open the app yourselfIf the car is already connected, Android Auto starts at once. If not, the normal home screen opens.
  • bluetooth_disabled
    The car disconnectsAfter a few seconds Android Auto stops and you are back on Andromi Auto's home screen, ready for the next drive.

high_qualityPicture quality

The picture is compressed twice: Android Auto turns its screen into a video, and HyperOS compresses the window again to send it to the car. A very high resolution therefore does not always look sharper.

  • tune
    Settings → Graphic → Resolution1080p or 1440p with the H.265 codec is the sensible range. Try both and keep what looks best on your car.
  • fact_check
    Settings → Android Auto pictureThe card at the top of Settings shows what Android Auto really sent last time. If it refused a size, the card says so.

system_updateUpdates

  1. Check for updates

    Open Settings and tap Check for updates. Andromi Auto looks for the newest stable release on GitHub.

  2. Download and install

    If there is a newer build, tap Download and install. The first time, Android asks you to allow installing apps from Andromi Auto: turn it on, come back and tap the button again.

info

Updates install over the old version and keep all your settings. You can also download any version from the releases page.

layersMetamodule (NoMount)

Our KernelSU modules add apps and settings files to the phone's system. KernelSU and KernelSU Next do not do that on their own any more: they need a metamodule, a special module that puts the other modules' files in place. Install it first, before the Interconnectivity and digital key modules. Without it they install, but their apps never show up.

We recommend NoMount. It makes module files appear without mounting anything, and it is what our modules are tested with.

downloadDownload NoMount helpWhat is a metamodule?
  1. Download NoMount

    On the NoMount release page, download the module zip under Assets.

  2. Install it in KernelSU

    Open KernelSU (or KernelSU Next) → Module → Install, choose the zip, then restart the phone.

  3. Then install our modules

    Continue with the Interconnectivity module below.

info

Only one metamodule can be active. If you already use another one (for example meta-overlayfs), it may work too, but NoMount is the one we test with.

NoMount works without a custom kernel on phones with a GKI kernel 5.10 or newer, which recent Xiaomi phones have.

extensionInterconnectivity module

On Global and EEA (EU) ROMs the phone cannot connect to a Xiaomi EV, so Andromi Auto has nowhere to be shown. The Xiaomi EV Interconnect KernelSU module adds Xiaomi's car connection service back to these ROMs, and sets the Wi-Fi country to China (CN): the phone connects to the car over Wi-Fi Direct, which only works on the Chinese Wi-Fi region, even on a Chinese ROM. This module is required: without it the app is of no use in the car.

downloadDownload the module codeModule source
  1. Install NoMount first

    If you have not yet, install the NoMount metamodule and restart.

  2. Download the zip

    On the release page, tap xiaomi-ev-interconnect-<version>.zip under Assets. Keep it as a zip; do not unpack it.

  3. Install it in KernelSU

    Open KernelSU (or KernelSU Next), go to the Module tab, tap Install and choose the zip.

  4. Reboot

    Restart the phone when KernelSU has finished. The phone can now connect to your Xiaomi EV with Interconnectivity.

info

Your phone's region and Wi-Fi country are China while the module is active, because Xiaomi's car connection only works on the Chinese region. Some Xiaomi apps that check the region may behave as on a Chinese ROM. Removing the module and restarting restores both.

No LSPosed needed. The module sets the Wi-Fi country by itself. If you used LSPosed or Vector with the "WiFi Country Code" module for this, turn that module off.

New versions of the module appear as an update in KernelSU's module list.

keyCar digital key

Not needed for Andromi Auto, but good to know if you are moving to a Global or EEA ROM: those ROMs cannot add your Xiaomi EV as a digital key on the phone. The Xiaomi EV Key Enabler KernelSU module adds the Digital Key and Smart cards apps that make it work.

downloadDownload the module codeModule source
  1. Download the zip

    On the release page, tap xiaomi-ev-key-<version>.zip under Assets. This module also needs the NoMount metamodule.

  2. Install it in KernelSU

    Open KernelSU → Module → Install, choose the zip, then restart the phone.

  3. Add the car key

    After the restart, add your Xiaomi EV as a digital key as you normally would.
