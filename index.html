Android Auto on your Xiaomi EV

Andromi Auto runs the real Android Auto on your Xiaomi phone and shows it in a window that HyperOS sends to the car's screen. Google Maps, your music and messages, right next to the car's own widgets.

Before you download

What you need

Check every required item first. Without them, Andromi Auto cannot reach the car screen.

Required

  • smartphone
    A Xiaomi phone on HyperOSAndromi Auto uses HyperOS's Xiaomi Interconnectivity to show its window on the car.
  • public
    Global or EEA (EU) ROM RecommendedThese ROMs come with Google services and support Android Auto, which the app runs on the phone.
  • lock_open
    Unlocked bootloader and rootRoot with KernelSU is needed to install the Interconnectivity module below, and it lets the app set up automatic start.
  • extension
    Interconnectivity moduleGlobal and EEA ROMs cannot connect to a Xiaomi EV on their own. The Xiaomi EV Interconnect KernelSU module adds it, and sets the Wi-Fi country to China, which the car connection needs. Without it, Andromi Auto has no car screen to show on. Install guide
  • play_circle
    The Android Auto appFrom Google Play, kept up to date.

Optional

  • key
    Car digital key moduleNot needed for Andromi Auto. Global and EEA ROMs cannot add a Xiaomi EV as a digital key; the Xiaomi EV Key Enabler KernelSU module brings it back. Install guide
warning

Unlocking the bootloader erases the phone. Back up your data first. Xiaomi also makes you wait before it allows the unlock, so plan ahead.

How it works

Your phone does the work, the car shows it

Xiaomi EVs do not support Android Auto themselves, but HyperOS can show a phone app on the car screen. Andromi Auto uses that: it starts Android Auto on the phone, displays it full size in its own window, and the car shows that window.

play_circle

1. Android Auto starts on the phone

Andromi Auto acts as the "head unit" Android Auto talks to, so Android Auto runs right on your phone. No dongle, no cable.

cast

2. HyperOS sends the window to the car

Xiaomi Interconnectivity shows the Andromi Auto window on the car's screen, where you pin it as a card in portrait or landscape.

touch_app

3. Drive with Android Auto

Use Google Maps, Waze, YouTube Music, Spotify, WhatsApp and every other Android Auto app by touch on the car screen.

Features

Made for Xiaomi cars and phones

bolt

Automatic start

Choose your car's Bluetooth and Android Auto starts by itself when you get in. When the car disconnects, Android Auto stops and the app waits for the next drive.

admin_panel_settings

One-tap root setup

On Android Auto 17.4 and newer, one tap re-enables what Android Auto needs to start by itself. No developer "head unit server" to start by hand.

screen_rotation

Portrait and landscape

One switch on the home screen: portrait for the car's tall window, landscape for the wide ones.

palette

Material You

Every screen follows Material 3 and your wallpaper colours, in light and dark mode, full screen without status or navigation bars.

high_quality

Picture you can check

Settings shows the resolution, codec and frame rate Android Auto really sent, up to 1440p and 4K with H.265.

system_update

Updates inside the app

Settings → Check for updates downloads the newest stable release from GitHub and installs it over the old one. Your settings stay.

Download

Get Andromi Auto

Make sure you have everything under What you need. Then download the APK on your phone and open it to install. Android asks once to allow installing from your browser. After that, Andromi Auto updates itself from Settings.
