About the app

What is Andromi Auto?

An Android app that runs the real Android Auto on your Xiaomi phone and shows it in a window. HyperOS sends that window to the screen of your Xiaomi EV, so you can use Android Auto in a car that does not support it on its own.

Is it made by Xiaomi or Google?

No. It is a free, open source community project based on Open Headunit. It is not affiliated with Xiaomi, Baidu or Google.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free and the full source code is on GitHub under the GNU AGPL v3.0 licence.

Can I use it without the car?

Yes. Tap Start Android Auto and Android Auto runs full screen on the phone. That is also the easiest way to check that everything works before you go to the car.

Phones, ROMs and root

Which phones does it work on?

Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones on HyperOS. HyperOS is required, because the app relies on Xiaomi Interconnectivity to appear on the car screen.

Which ROM should I use: Chinese, Global or EEA?

A Global or EEA (EU) ROM is ideal. Android Auto runs on the phone, so it needs Google services and a ROM where Android Auto is supported, which these ROMs have.

The catch: Global and EEA ROMs cannot connect to a Xiaomi EV out of the box. A KernelSU module adds that back, so you also need an unlocked bootloader and root. See the Interconnectivity module section of the guide.

Do I need root?

Yes. You need an unlocked bootloader and root with KernelSU. On a Global or EEA ROM the phone cannot connect to a Xiaomi EV without the Interconnectivity module, and that module is installed through KernelSU. Without it Andromi Auto has no car screen to show on.

Root is also what lets the app set up automatic start with one tap.

Unlocking the bootloader erases the phone, so back up your data first.

Will I lose the car's digital key on a Global or EEA ROM?

Out of the box, Global and EEA ROMs cannot add a Xiaomi EV as a digital key. It has nothing to do with Andromi Auto, but the Xiaomi EV Key Enabler KernelSU module brings this support back. How to install it.

Why does my phone show China as its region after installing the module?

Xiaomi's car connection service only runs on the Chinese region, so the Xiaomi EV Interconnect module reports the region as China while it is active. Some Xiaomi apps that check the region may behave as on a Chinese ROM. Removing the module and restarting restores the original region.

Why does it install as com.baidu.searchbox?

The car only shows phone apps whose package name is on Xiaomi's allowed list, so Andromi Auto uses the name of an app on that list.

That means it cannot be installed next to the real Baidu app or another app using the same name. It is also why Andromi Auto will never be on Google Play: download it from the releases page.

Using it

Andromi Auto does not appear on the car screen

Check that the phone is connected to the car with Xiaomi Interconnectivity and that other phone apps appear on the car. On a Global or EEA ROM, the Interconnectivity module must be installed.

Also make sure the real Baidu app is not installed: the car would show that one instead.

Android Auto does not start

Update Android Auto from Google Play and open it once on the phone to accept its terms. Then start it again from Andromi Auto.

If automatic start does nothing, open Automatic start: all three items (root setup, car Bluetooth, open from the background) must show a check mark, and the switch must be on.

What happens when I leave the car?

With automatic start on, Android Auto stops a few seconds after the car's Bluetooth disconnects and Andromi Auto goes back to its home screen. When the car connects again, Android Auto starts again by itself.

The picture is squeezed or has black bars

Match the orientation to the car window: on the home screen, use the Screen switch. Portrait for the car's tall window (size 1), landscape for the wide ones (sizes 2 and 3).

Why isn't the picture as sharp as on the phone?

The picture is compressed twice: once by Android Auto and once by HyperOS when it sends the window to the car. A higher resolution does not always help. 1080p or 1440p with H.265 (Settings → Graphic → Resolution) works best. Improving this is ongoing work.

How do I go back? There is no back arrow.

Use the phone's back gesture. The app runs full screen without status or navigation bars, so on some phones the first swipe shows the bars and the second one goes back.

Updates

How do I update?

Open Settings and tap Check for updates. The newest stable release is downloaded and installed over the old version. Your settings stay. The first time, Android asks you to allow installing apps from Andromi Auto.

What are the development builds on the releases page?

Test builds with the newest changes, marked "Pre-release". They are for testing and may have problems. The update check in the app only offers stable releases.
